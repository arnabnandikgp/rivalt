## Resource envelope and continuation policy

Two maximum-width row calculations exceeded the existing headroom target before
context overhead. Verify and FlowPlan now take exactly one row per transaction;
complete greatest/fixed-rate and insurance checks have their own finalization.
Value/Denominator/Apply/Reconcile retain canonical one/two-row pages. Storage stays
two rows per page; exact economic equations, membership, 1.2M CU ceiling, 32 KiB
heap and 20% headroom remain unchanged.

Qualified `915d8e8` executes 534 separately labelled full-context resource probes.
They fork a genuine 64-root runtime, widen only derived arithmetic scratch to
5,632-bit L and 186-bit factor, and include maximum-cash/claim inputs. They verify
actual proposal, row, finalization, ordering and bounded circulation instructions.
Peak observed continuation: 774,367 CU. Packets remain below 1,232 bytes.
Final RPC peaks are 823,235 CU for sampling, 101,133 for full-book liquidation,
49,170 for initial activation and 1,176 bytes per packet. All operations retain
the required headroom under their existing declared budgets.

These scratch probes deliberately do not represent a canonical financial history.
No Apply or token movement occurs; financial accounts and the parent runtime remain
unchanged. Independent BigInt formulas check exact flows/floors. The separate
arithmetic fixture also exercises actual Rust functions with maximum magnitudes.
Together with signed complete-cohort financial tests, this measures bounded work;
it is not a claim that a real maximum-value 64-trader portfolio was constructed.

Default artifacts reject activation, liquidation, freeze, rate finalization,
recovery/reactivation, retirement and reserve withdrawal. Initial local activation
requires every safety page and fresh Normal sources/makers; old candidate activation
cannot bypass it. Partial Apply cash remains unspendable until complete reconciliation.
Resolved/recovered-custody/retired owner cash exits do not require artificial new
source freshness. Insurance can leave only after irreversible retirement and only
to the immutable canonical reserve destination.
