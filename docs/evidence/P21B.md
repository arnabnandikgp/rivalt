## Exit limit and capital comparison

**Continuous sampling prevented all three two-transaction exits.** Twelve proof
transactions finalized (four per account), but intervening group updates invalidated
the certificate before the separate withdrawal. After explicitly stopping publication,
funding and maker workers with all inventory flat, three new proof/withdrawal pairs
finalized and transferred one atom each. This proves admissible quiescent token
exits, not always-available busy-group exits or complete capital redemption.

Proof members are deliberately read-only; combining a page with a withdrawal would
promote a member writable across the transaction and violate the selected contract.
This PR preserves that authority and the sequence/source guards. Operations must
expose certification invalidation and use an explicit coordinated maintenance
window for this test profile. A live-service exit-availability redesign requires
separately scoped protocol/SDK acceptance before funded use; no silent sampler
pause, longer source lifetime or weaker proof is claimed as a fix.

Equal combined trader capital is 1,000,000 atoms for cross and 500,000 each for the
two isolated accounts. Real unsigned 100-lot probes at the same prices/10% IM/5% MM
accept BTC in cross and reject BTC in its isolated account; ETH is admitted in both.
The other isolated 500,000 cannot guarantee BTC. This demonstrates allocation
flexibility with zero positive unpaid credit or correlation discount. No external
maker economics, hedge result, calibrated funded-risk claim or new asset is implied.

P15B/P16B remain Open external gates. Continuous 24-hour endurance stays Deferred.
The JavaScript bundle remains about 717 kB (208 kB gzip), with the existing Vite
