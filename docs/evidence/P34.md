## Default, recovery and complete funded exits

The fresh operational run opens cross-market exposure, executes a real consenting
funded liquidation with strict post-fee progress, then freezes all ten registered
roots after fresh stress. The production risk worker uploads/checks the exact
proposal, survives SIGKILL during a partial upload, and resumes bounded ordering,
rounding, application and verification. It performs sustained recovery checks;
only the venue authority resumes. The independent raw-account Fraction model
recreates all ten payouts and rejects five corrupted cash/ledger/vault states.

All 90 durable service originals finalize successfully: 72 risk and 18 funding.
After recovery, all ten roots are flat and every remaining funded balance exits;
all root cash/claims become zero. The vault retains 452 atoms attributable only
to market insurance/protocol fees. Fresh PostgreSQL reconstruction is identical,
with zero unexplained surplus. Signed originals, transactions, raw snapshots,
worker logs and reconciliation reports remain ignored/local.

The first operational run exposed a profile-3-only service proposal reader. The
fix requires the runtime’s exact configured profile 3/4 while retaining schema-3
layouts and all canonical ownership/cohort/collection/denominator checks.
Eight focused profile/risk tests and the fresh full runtime qualify this fix.
