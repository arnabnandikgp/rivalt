## Operational limits

See [the walkthrough](../P28A-LOCAL-DEMO.md). This is loopback-only, uses fixed
synthetic BTC/ETH prices, internal makers and valueless collateral, with one DB,
bounded logs and a 60-minute lease. Startup requires more than 1 GiB free; shutdown
starts below 768 MiB. About 950 MiB of rebuildable compiler caches was reclaimed
after the disk-guard stop; qualified artifacts and all evidence were preserved.

The actual Surfpool restart probe preserves balances/history but changes genesis
and resets slots, so stop ends the chain and reset deliberately creates another.
Reconciliation can briefly make indexed history unavailable; fail-closed readiness
and direct-RPC balance/exit behavior remain unchanged. Unpaid-profit payment still
needs current certified backing. No funded liquidation receiver or forced-default
walkthrough is invented. P15B/P16B stay Open and 24-hour endurance stays Deferred.
