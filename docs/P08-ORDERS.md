# P08 persistent orders and existing-position protection

A11/A12/G09 pass on the clean revisions recorded in [P08 acceptance](evidence/P08.md); integration status remains in WORKLOG. This extends only the explicit local-test-profile, in authenticated market mode 2. The default build rejects order activation/execution. Experimental layouts may change; no upgrade migration is implied.

Four bounded records live inside each Participant, with two protective-group records and checked monotonically allocated IDs. Terminal records remain until slot reuse; events preserve history. Independent orders occupy one slot; a TP/SL pair occupies two and shares one group allowance. Keepers cannot create wallet authority. Immediate routes retain their owner signer; stored-order execution is a separate instruction binding participant, order ID, version and exact cumulative filled quantity.

| G09 parameter | Local selection |
|---|---|
| Authorized total | 1–4,608 lots per order; every submitted subfill is exact-base atomic, at most two makers |
| Lifetime | Inclusive start / exclusive expiry in slots; 1–216,000-slot duration and expiry at most 216,000 slots from placement/replacement |
| Price | Per-lot all-in buy ceiling / sell floor, 9,000–110,000 atoms; exact aggregate cross-products include incremental trading fee |
| Fee budget | Explicit lifetime cap at most 1,000,000 atoms; replacement preserves cumulative filled quantity, notional and ceil-fee prefix |
| Trigger | None, central reference at/above, or at/below; trigger 10,000–100,000 atoms; valid current index-derived reference checked on every attempt |
| Reservation | Conservative sum of remaining execution-loss, incremental IM and remaining authorized fee budget; no exclusivity discount is assumed |
| Keeper | Separately funded 5,000,000-lamport operator cap; preferred subfill at most 64 lots; existing immutable queue/reservation contract applies |

For each remaining risk-increasing buy lot, reserve `ceil(upper/10) + max(limit-lower,0)`; for a sell, `ceil(upper/10) + max(upper-limit,0)`. Add the unspent lifetime fee budget. Per-lot upward rounding keeps the bound conservative across partial fills. Reduce-only orders add no directional IM but retain the execution-loss and fee reservation. Protective quantities are clipped by remaining child quantity, shared group allowance and current absolute position. Sum children conservatively even though the group enforces mutual quantity consumption.

Recompute reservations from current bounds after fills and before sensitive admission, gain settlement, withdrawals and funding-depth qualification. An observation does not scan every participant. Stored reserves have an as-of observation; previews expose current required reserve separately. Cancellation/expiry cleanup needs no source or maker CPI and releases only remaining stored authority. When credible bounds are unavailable, sensitive actions reserve against the entire finite 1–1,000,000-atom risk range until the owner cancels or valid bounds return. A market jump can make an order unfillable; a reservation is not an execution guarantee.

Partial fills keep the order version. Cancel targets its current ID/version. Replace advances version and preserves the executed prefix; a stale cancel cannot cancel the replacement. Original quantity remains historical; the newly authorized current total must exceed the already-filled quantity. Checked IDs prevent slot reuse from reviving an old instruction. Current maker/config epochs are validated on each subfill; maker renewal does not reset continuing user limits or fee budgets. Market terms epochs remain bound to stored authority.

Protection requires an existing nonzero position. At creation the TP and SL thresholds lie on opposite sides of the current central reference, with directions determined by the position sign. Shared original allowance cannot exceed current absolute exposure and never grows automatically. A partial TP consumes the allowance available to SL. Manual reductions clip current eligibility. Flat/flip invalidates the generation; reopening cannot revive protection. Cancelling one child leaves the sibling valid; explicit version-bound group cancellation cancels both. Child replacement advances the group version, so an older group cancellation cannot erase changed authority. Replacement cannot grow group allowance or change its generation/trigger direction.

States are Empty, Open, PartiallyFilled, Filled, Cancelled, Expired and Invalidated. Chain order decides races. No executing lock, trigger latch, fill reward, priority promise, general IOC, trailing stop or entry-linked bracket is introduced. Order events and execution snapshots rebuild through the same finalized SQL checkpoint contract; failed transactions never create order progress.

The generated layout is 815 bytes per Participant, including a 634-byte book (four 132-byte orders and two 41-byte groups plus counters). No new order PDAs expand the RPC snapshot account set. Keeper discovery excludes its own maker capital from the quote candidates and tries at most seven descending sizes from 64 to one lot when capacity or fee budget prevents its preferred fill. With all twelve trader/maker records populated, the signed split uses 298,776 CU / 824 bytes / 18 accounts; actual keeper execution reaches 296,723 CU / 18 accounts. This passes the unchanged 320,000-CU / 1,232-byte / 20-account ceiling. The ten real RPC/WS/PostgreSQL acceptance checks and source identities are in P08 acceptance.
