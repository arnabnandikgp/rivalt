# Venue qualification scorecard

These findings track what has been demonstrated on a particular deployment and
what it would take to extend that result. The fresh BTC/ETH demo, the wider local
venue and standalone width fixtures have different binaries and acceptance bounds.
[Deployment summary](../STATUS.md)

## Findings to revisit

| # | Finding and status | Strongest evidence | Closure criterion or remaining limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Position storage · **Closed** | On clearing `b7e4458b` and harness `d175285e`, an internal maker carried inventory through 80 mixed-price round trips. All 163 signed routes landed and the owner withdrew. [Storage measurement](performance.md#fixed-width-maker-entry) | Fixed 40-byte records bounded account growth across those fills. Entry-cost rounding can affect payable amounts. The accepted bounds are part of the [accounting contract](../clearing/accounting.md#ordinary-payouts-on-closed-quantity). |
| 2 | Published versus executable depth · **Closed for the bounded local workload** | The eight-leg wallet filled 8/10 requests at each of 16 and 32 registered makers on the matched trace. Separate curved-price and margin-bound orders reached their independently computed four-leg optima. The reviewed change merged in [PR #191](https://github.com/arnabnandikgp/rivalt/pull/191). [Depth and limits](performance.md#atomic-fills-across-three-to-eight-makers) | Qualification covered funded allocations, signed prices and fees, and atomic refusal. Restricted shortlists can miss a better maker. Execution still requires full admission. |
| 3 | Wallet count and throughput · **Closed for the bounded local workload** | Surfpool filled 288/288 matched requests. A separate Agave run landed all 50 pre-signed orders in one slot in each of two placements. The default SDK decoder passed independent byte and wallet-state replay. [Contention measurements](performance.md#wallet-count-and-market-contention) | Pre-signed bursts and wallet preparation have separate denominators. Sustained demand and priority-fee competition remain unmeasured. The 200 ms repeat is deferred. |
| 4 | Closed profit to wallet cash · **Closed for bounded local BTC/ETH** | On clearing `e4aff314` and harness `11cb1b55`, the worker paid two 40-atom gains on a 1,000-member book. The funded owner withdrew both. The underfunded debtor blocked payment through restart and membership replacement. [Worker qualification](performance.md#payout-with-incremental-debtor-groups) | Both exits completed during finite competing traffic without a membership walk. Borderline groups can still require exact debtor checks. |
| 5 | Default complexity at a given population · **Deferred to the shock exercise** | Three 75-member books needed 193, 285 and 327 finalized keeper transactions. On a separate 128-member book one payout calculation needed 471 continuations. [Recovery results](performance.md#default-and-wallet-exits) | Hold population fixed and vary debt concentration, cross-market claims and eligible reductions. Measure work to completion, progress after restart and time to the last wallet exit. |
| 6 | Scripted flow versus wallet behavior · **Open** | In the 72-order control, each of 16 wallets used just one size. The 24-wallet burst was BTC buy-only. [Comparison recipe](local-development.md#wallet-load-comparison) | Vary arrival, size, side and market within each wallet. Include repeat traders, reductions, reversals and outstanding orders. Preserve one common trace across comparisons and repeat with additional seeds. |
| 7 | Coordinated maker and oracle updates · **Open** | The existing fixture can refresh six maker publications and the synthetic sources together. The funding publisher measures published depth. [Fixture](../../tests/rpc-contract/liquidity-execution-setup.ts) · [Funding contract](../liquidity/funding.md) | Replay the Finding 6 trace with independent maker and oracle clocks. Measure executable spread, refusal reasons and funding coverage. Reserve the combined price jump and maker outage for the shock exercise. |
| 8 | Willing liquidation capital · **Deferred to the shock exercise** | On `5d7a7c0f`, a maker took ten lots and an operator-owned receiver took five after it was funded. The deficit reached zero with 35 lots remaining. [Signed qualification](https://github.com/arnabnandikgp/rivalt/pull/143) | Make several unhealthy accounts compete for limited receiver capital. Measure deficit after fees, remaining exposure and recovery time. Independent receiver participation requires separate evidence. |
| 9 | Sustainable participant costs · **Open** | A local burst filled 119/120 offers and earned 2,963 collateral atoms in taker fees before insurance. Wallets paid zero priority fees and all makers were internal. [Cost baseline](performance.md#funding-costs-and-comparisons) | Report actual lamports per role, retained fees after insurance and funded versus unpaid maker P&L. Label hedge and priority-fee assumptions explicitly. |
| 10 | More markets on one cross account · **Open for general admission** | A sparse fixture filled eight markets. The funded venue separately executed BTC/ETH/SOL on one account and verified its maximum withdrawal and transfers with all three positions open. [Width and custody measurements](performance.md#market-width-and-cash-access) | Preserve whole-account funding, reservations, cash limits, liquidation and complete recovery for every book the venue admits. The listed venue can still reach books outside the default verifier's bounds. |
| 11 | Default across three or more markets · **Closed for the bounded local venue** | On clearing `69c2f5d5` and harness `49623912`, both 12-member BTC/ETH/SOL books settled. Each had ten actual wallet exits and reconciled all 210 accounts. [Insured and residual outcomes](performance.md#default-and-wallet-exits) | Closure covers the funded three-market book and its verified insurance and loss allocation. Four-market and fractional-rounding fixtures exercised separate cases. General wider admission remains Finding 10. [Verifier bounds](../clearing/default-plan-verification.md#default-on-a-funded-venue-with-a-listed-market) |
